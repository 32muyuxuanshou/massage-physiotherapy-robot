## Lighting

Poly Haven, [studio_small_03](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_03), 1K HDRI, [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Only this HDRI from the material exploration downloads was used in the final rendering. The downloaded cloth textures were not substituted for the scanned photographic textures.

## Papers and software

Paper titles/URLs/hashes are in PAPER_RECEIPT.json; full PDFs are local research references, not redistributed here. Blender is the renderer; custom perspective depth and QA code is in the project. We do not claim to have reproduced BEDLAM/SynBody's entire synthesis systems.
